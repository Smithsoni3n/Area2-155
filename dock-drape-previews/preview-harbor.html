Bremerton Workforce Archive · 1960s to 1970s

PSNS Dungarees · Early 60s

Puget Sound Naval Shipyard was Bremerton's top employer, and blue chambray + denim dungarees were standard yard wear.

Clinical Whites · Early 60s

Crisp nursing whites symbolized discipline; starch and polished shoes matched the military-adjacent hospital culture.

Service Bay Gear · Late 60s

In Bremerton's car-heavy corridors, attendants wore branded shirts, caps, and grease-resistant work slacks.

Retail & Stewardess Style

Structured dresses, neck scarves, and polished grooming mirrored Bremerton's expanding service economy and travel ties.

Maritime / Logging Rugged

Oilskin coats, denim, and heavy boots dominated maritime and logging routes feeding Kitsap's industrial chain.

Shipyard Coveralls · 70s

Women in technical and yard support roles adopted heavy-duty coveralls built for welding zones and wet weather.
