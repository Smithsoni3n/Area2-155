Kitsap Vintage Fashion 1960s–70s

This educational page introduces style shifts from the 1960s into the 1970s. During years shaped by the Vietnam War, civil rights activism, the hippie movement, and the Nixon era, clothing became a powerful way for people to express identity, protest, freedom, and social change.
