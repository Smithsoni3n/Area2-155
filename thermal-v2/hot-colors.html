HOT THERMAL BAND

Red • Orange • Pink Psychology

Warm thermal frequencies are high-energy visual signals. They increase urgency, action tendency, and emotional intensity in both environmental and interface design.

Red = Alert + Arousal

Elliot & Maier (2014) summarize that red can increase vigilance and physiological activation in evaluative contexts.

Orange = Momentum + Social Heat

Orange hues often communicate movement and extroversion, making them effective for dynamic CTA zones.

Pink = Social Safety + Empathy

Pink balances heat with emotional softness. In typography systems, pink accents can preserve urgency while reducing perceived aggression.
