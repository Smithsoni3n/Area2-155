EXPLAINED

Thermal cameras detect infrared radiation invisible to the human eye and map temperature differences to a visible color spectrum. This false-color visualization reveals the metabolic heat signature of living creatures in their environment.

BLUE/PURPLE — Cool zones 20–30°C. Ambient air, foliage, extremities.
GREEN/YELLOW — Moderate 30–40°C. Outer body surface, resting zones.
RED/ORANGE — Hot zones 40–50°C. Active muscles, face, core.
