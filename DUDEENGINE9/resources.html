Handler focus

The vest

A clear, labeled vest is the first line of defense against public interference. It signals "I am working" to the world.

Features reflective patches for visibility and secure attachment points for ID tags.

Boots

Protects paws from hot asphalt in summer and road salt in winter. Healthy paws keep a working dog moving.

Durable rubber soles with adjustable straps ensure comfort during long shifts.

Field first aid

A canine-specific kit with gauze, vet wrap, and hydrogen peroxide rides with us on every trip.

Includes emergency contact info for our vet and instructions for common injuries.

ADA info cards

Small handouts that explain federal access rights to business owners who may not know the law.

Reduces confrontations and educates the public about service dog regulations.

Hydration pack

Collapsible water bowl and fresh water supply ensure Dude stays hydrated during outings.

Essential for maintaining focus and physical well-being during extended work periods.

Training log

Digital and paper records track training sessions, behavioral patterns, and medical alerts.

Helps identify patterns and document Dude's ongoing development as a service dog.
