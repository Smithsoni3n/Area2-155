How it works
01 — Harvest

Thermoelectric generators woven into fabric convert the temperature gradient between your skin (~98°F) and ambient air (~68°F) into a continuous electrical current. No moving parts. Fully passive.

02 — Store & Route

Micro-capacitors in wristbands and collar patches buffer harvested energy. Smart routing firmware decides: charge your devices first, then relay surplus into the cooperative mesh network via Bluetooth/NFC.

03 — Contribute

Surplus flows to city micro-grid nodes. At 3–4 billion participants, individual milliwatts aggregate into gigawatts — enough to power hospitals, schools, and emergency services with zero fuel cost.
