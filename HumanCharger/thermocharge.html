How it works
01
Harvest
Thermoelectric generators woven into fabric use the temperature gap between your skin (~98°F) and ambient air to produce a steady electrical current. Fully passive — no effort required.
02
Store & Route
Micro-capacitors in wristbands buffer the harvested energy. Smart firmware routes power to your devices first, then relays surplus energy to the city grid via the mesh network.
03
Contribute
At 3–4 billion participants, individual milliwatts add up to gigawatts — enough to supplement hospitals, schools, and emergency services with zero fuel cost.
