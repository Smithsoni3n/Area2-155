"Distraction is Dangerous."
The Rules of Engagement.

Respect the Vest

When you see a dog in a vest, you aren't looking at a pet. You are looking at medical equipment with a heartbeat. It might look like he is just "standing there," but he is working. He is monitoring my heart rate, my breathing, and the environment.

Do Not Touch

Even a quick pat on the head breaks his focus. Imagine if someone unplugged your computer while you were working. That is what distraction does to a service dog.

Do Not Talk

Making kissy noises, whistling, or calling his name forces him to choose between his training and his instinct to be friendly. Don't make him choose.
