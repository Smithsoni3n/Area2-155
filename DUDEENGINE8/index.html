Our Journey Begins

For years, I built walls. Life threw challenges at me—complicated family dynamics, relationships that drained my spirit, and the overwhelming weight of trying to fix everyone else's problems while ignoring my own. I became an expert at "keeping busy," a survival tactic to avoid the silence where the pain lived.

Then came the day my friend brought home this puppy. It wasn't just "cute." It was chemical. From the moment I met him, the air in the room felt lighter. He didn't ask me to fix anything. He didn't need me to be strong. He just needed me to be. He manages to lift my mood and make me smile, pulling me out of that anxious place I called home for so long.

Last year, when my mom passed away, the grief was a physical weight. I remember sitting on my front porch, staring at the memorial I built in the yard, feeling a loneliness so deep it echoed. And there was Dude. Not running around, not barking—just leaning his weight against my leg. He knew. That silent, solid presence saved my life. He is my Anchor.
