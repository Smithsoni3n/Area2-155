DOCK ↔ DRAPE

PSNS Dungarees

Puget Sound Naval Shipyard crews wore denim dungarees and utility shirts for mechanical labor in Bremerton.

Clinical Nursing Whites

Navy and community medical staff wore starched white uniforms that reflected strict hygiene standards.

Service Station Uniform

Late-60s attendant uniforms reflected full-service fueling culture as commuting and car ownership expanded.

Retail / Stewardess Tailoring

Women in service roles wore coordinated tailoring designed for presentation, movement, and long shifts.

Maritime / Logging Gear

Wool layers, waxed outerwear, and heavy boots were essential for wet industrial Northwest work.

Shipyard Coveralls

By the 1970s, more women entered industrial trades and used protective coveralls for fabrication and dockside tasks.
