Nitro Section: Thermal Vision + Color Psychology

Thermal cameras translate infrared energy into false-color maps: deep blues/cyans for cooler zones and yellow-red-white for heat concentration. In wildlife tracking, this reveals muscle activity, respiration cues, and stress states. In psychology, warm colors increase urgency and arousal while cool tones lower cognitive strain. This site fuses both systems into a typographic learning lab.
