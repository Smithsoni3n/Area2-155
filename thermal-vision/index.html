Thermal Vision + Color Psychology

Thermal imagery maps energy into color gradients; human cognition maps color to meaning. Research shows warm hues can elevate perceived excitement and urgency, while cool hues support perceived calmness and cognitive control. This project combines psychophysiology, design theory, and visual rhetoric for a typography-forward exploration of thermal color language.
