Intro: Thermal Vision + Color Psychology

Thermal cameras translate infrared energy into false-color palettes. In jungle monitoring, gorillas and big cats become living heat maps: warmer cores often map toward red/orange while cooler limbs and environment shift toward cyan/blue. This site connects those thermal signatures to psychological responses documented in environmental and color-emotion research.

Hot Colors

Red, orange, magenta and arousal, urgency, risk, appetite, and dominance signals.

Cold Colors

Blue, cyan, violet and calmness, trust, restoration, and cognitive stabilization.
