HOT THERMAL BAND

Red • Orange • Pink Psychology

In thermal maps, these colors mark higher heat zones. In human design psychology, warm wavelengths frequently raise activation and urgency when used with contrast and spatial rhythm.

Red = Arousal + Alert

Elliot & Maier (2014) report red can heighten vigilance and increase physiological arousal in performance settings.

Orange = Energy + Motion

Lab findings in attention studies show orange can support perceived activity and social warmth in dynamic interfaces.

Pink = Social Safety + Compassion

Pink shifts warmth from aggressive to empathetic. When paired with deep navy, it supports modern typography hierarchy while retaining high emotional visibility. This makes it powerful for call-to-action systems in educational design.
