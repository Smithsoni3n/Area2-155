First Christmas in Heaven

Dear Mom,

This Christmas feels different without you here. The chair where you sat, the laugh that filled the room, the way you made everything feel like home.

I keep thinking about that last meal I made for you—how Dad finally said yes after seven days, and I got to take care of you one more time. I didn't know it would be our goodbye, but I'm so grateful it happened.

I know you're watching over us now, probably cheering on the Packers from the best seat in the house.

Until we meet again, we carry you with us—in every tradition, every memory, every moment of love.

Merry Christmas, Mom.
We love you forever. 💕
