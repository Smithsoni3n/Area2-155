My Anchor

I'm not a victim—I'm a believer in better. PTSD doesn't care about strength. Dude became my anchor in those moments.

When flashbacks hit, when hypervigilance makes the world feel unsafe—he's there. He grounds me.

His steady breathing becomes mine.

My Anchor

He's saved my life more times than I can count. Not in dramatic, visible ways—but in the quiet moments when I couldn't find my way back. When the weight of trauma felt too heavy. When isolation threatened to swallow me whole.

This website is my tribute to Dude, and my message to anyone else struggling with PTSD, trauma, or the aftermath of domestic violence: You are not alone. There is hope. There is healing. And sometimes, it comes with four paws and an unbreakable spirit.
