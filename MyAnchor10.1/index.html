Our Story

For years, my life felt like a relentless battle. I went through a gauntlet of family conflicts and tough relationships that left me feeling completely alone against a mountain of hurt. I spent years just keeping busy and taking care of everyone else, just so I wouldn't have to face the quiet pain inside. Even after I broke free, it was hard to let anyone in, so I kept most of my story a secret.

Things started to change when a friend brought home a puppy, and the moment Dude stepped into my life, everything felt lighter. He never asked about my history. He just offered a presence that was immediately, undeniably comforting. He's the one who pulls me out of that anxious place and reminds me what a real, honest smile feels like.

When the unthinkable happened—losing my mom last year—my world was different. I made her memorial in the front yard, and Dude was always right there, my quiet guardian. He just knew. He'd listen in his way, a solid presence supporting me through the grief. There were days I could only sit on my front porch, feeling totally alone with nobody to talk to, and Dude was all I had.

That's when it hit me. His presence and the comfort he gives are exactly what makes a service animal. He didn't just walk into my life. He anchored it. He saved my life, and he continues to fight that battle alongside me, every single day. This is more than a bond. It is a lifeline forged in fire.

A Believer in Better

I'm not a victim—I'm a believer in better. My journey through domestic violence and trauma taught me strength I didn't know I had. But PTSD doesn't care about strength.

Dude became my anchor in those moments. When triggers send my heart racing, his steady breathing becomes mine. Deep pressure therapy from his weight against me brings me back to the present.

This website is my tribute to Dude, and my message to anyone else struggling with PTSD, trauma, or the aftermath of domestic violence: You are not alone. There is hope. There is healing. And sometimes, it comes with four paws and an unbreakable spirit.
