PTSD & Trauma Resources

National Center for PTSD

Part of the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs, provides research, education, and training on PTSD and traumatic stress.

Website: ptsd.va.gov

Resources: Self-help tools, treatment information, mobile apps, and family support.

The National Alliance on Mental Illness

NAMI provides advocacy, education, support, and public awareness for individuals and families affected by mental illness.

Helpline: 1-800-950-NAMI (6264)

Website: nami.org

Domestic Violence Resources

National Domestic Violence Hotline: 1-800-799-7233 (SAFE) or text START to 88788. Available 24/7 in over 200 languages.

The National Coalition Against Domestic Violence: Resources, statistics, and advocacy. Website: ncadv.org

Safety Planning: If you're in danger, create a safety plan. Include emergency contacts, important documents, and a safe place to go.
