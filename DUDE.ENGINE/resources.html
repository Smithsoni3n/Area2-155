Handler Resources

Essential Gear & Focus for the Service Dog Team.

Handler Focus

The Vest

A clear, labeled vest is the first line of defense against public interference. It signals "I am working" to the world.

Boots

Essential for protecting paws from hot asphalt in summer and road salt in winter. Healthy paws mean a working dog.

Field First Aid

I always carry a kit specific to canine trauma: gauze, vet wrap, and hydrogen peroxide.

ADA Info Cards

Small handouts that explain federal access rights to business owners who may not know the law.
