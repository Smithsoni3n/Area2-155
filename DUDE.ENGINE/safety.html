Story Facts Safety

Important safety considerations when interacting with or working around Dude.

Public Etiquette

Do not approach or touch a working service animal without asking the handler first. Respect their instructions and always maintain appropriate distance. Even friendly gestures can distract and endanger a working dog.

Handler Commands & Environment

Maintain a quiet, focused environment when around service animals. Secure loose items, avoid sudden gestures, and never attempt to feed or distract the animal. Handlers rely on their dogs for safety and stability.
